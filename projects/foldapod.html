FoldaPod: Origami-Inspired Privacy Pod

2026 Final Year Project Team of 4

Project Overview

FoldaPod is an origami-inspired deployable privacy pod designed for women and girls in crisis zones, refugee camps, and emergency shelters. The project combines humanitarian design principles with advanced mechanical engineering, utilizing bistable origami structures, CAD modeling, FEA simulation, and prototype fabrication.

Key Features

  • Compact folded profile: 120 x 50 x 10 cm for efficient transport
  • Rapid deployment in under 3 minutes without tools
  • Approximately 1 m² of usable private space
  • Minimum height of 1.8 m for standing use
  • Wind resistance verified up to 15 km/h
  • Designed for 200+ deployment cycles

Engineering Design

Origami Engineering Principles

The design leverages multistable origami principles from Harvard research (Melancon et al., 2021), adapted for low-cost humanitarian applications:

  • Rigid Facets: Stiff triangular panels that maintain structural integrity
  • Compliant Fold Lines: Controlled scoring for repeated folding
  • Bistable States: Stable folded and deployed configurations
  • Geometric Compatibility: Mathematical validation of deployment path

CAD Design & Fabrication

Design Workflow

  1. SolidWorks 3D modeling for form verification and visualization
  2. AutoCAD 2D drafting for laser cutting patterns
  3. Material selection: 2mm corrugated polypropylene sheet
  4. Laser cutting and scoring for precise fold lines
  5. Assembly and deployment testing
